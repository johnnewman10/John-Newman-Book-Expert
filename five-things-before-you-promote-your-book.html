Five Things to Check Before You Promote Your Book

Promotion only pays off if the page readers land on does its job. Before spending time or money, review five things.

First, your cover: does it signal your genre at thumbnail size? Second, your description: does the opening line give a reader a reason to keep going? Third, your categories and keywords: do they match where your readers browse?

Fourth, your price and format options. Fifth, your next step for readers, such as a mailing list or the next book. Fix these first and every promotion works harder.
