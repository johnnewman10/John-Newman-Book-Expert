Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how I work.

Who do you work with?

Authors and publishers of all kinds: first-time, established, self-published, traditionally published, independent presses and publishing companies.

Do you work with first-time authors?

Yes. Many clients are publishing their first book, and I explain each step in plain language.

Do you market books across all genres?

Yes. The strategy changes by genre because readers discover books differently.

Do you work with self-published authors?

Yes, including authors publishing through Amazon KDP.

Do you work with traditionally published authors?

Yes. I can support your publisher's efforts with additional author-led marketing.

Do you offer worldwide services?

Yes. I am based in the United States and work fully remotely with clients worldwide.

What book marketing services do you provide?

Amazon and KDP promotion, launches, ethical review campaigns, social and paid advertising, branding, email, SEO, design and consulting. See the Services page for all 21.

Do you offer customized packages?

Yes. Packages are shaped around your goals, genre, publishing stage and needs.

How does pricing work?

I do not publish fixed prices. After a consultation I send a custom quote for your scope.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Requests are pending until I personally confirm a time with you by email.

How long does a marketing campaign take?

It depends on the goals and services. Launches often run several weeks; ongoing work runs month to month.

Do you provide progress reports?

Yes. You receive regular updates and performance reports during the campaign.

Can you market an already published book?

Yes. Older books can often be repositioned and promoted again.

Can you help with a new book launch?

Yes. The earlier we start before release, the more we can prepare.

How do I get started?

Request a free consultation or send an inquiry through the contact page.
