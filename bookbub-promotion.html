BookBub Promotion

Preparation and positioning for BookBub featured deals and ads.

What is bookbub promotion?

Preparation and positioning for BookBub featured deals and ads. Every engagement is shaped around your book, genre, audience and publishing stage rather than a fixed formula.

Why authors need it

Readers cannot buy a book they never see. Good writing alone rarely gets a book noticed, and this service helps close the gap between a finished book and the readers who would enjoy it.

Common problems it solves

  • Your book is hard to find
  • Promotion that reaches the wrong readers
  • Unclear or inconsistent messaging
  • No clear plan for what to do next

What my service includes

  • A review of your book and current position
  • A tailored bookbub promotion plan
  • Preparation and delivery of the agreed work
  • Progress updates and a performance report

How it works

  • Free consultation to discuss your goals
  • Assessment and recommended approach
  • Custom quote and agreement
  • Delivery, monitoring and reporting

Potential benefits

  • Better visibility with relevant readers
  • A clearer, more confident marketing plan
  • More opportunities to sell
  • Results are never guaranteed, but the work is done with care and transparency

Who should use it

  • First-time and established authors
  • Self-published and traditionally published authors
  • Independent publishers and publishing companies

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee results from bookbub promotion?

No. I do not guarantee sales, rankings or reviews. I do guarantee honest work and clear reporting.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is a custom quote based on your book and goals. Request one and I will reply by email.

Can I combine this with other services?

Yes. Many authors combine services into a package.
