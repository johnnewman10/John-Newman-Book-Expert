My journey in book marketing

For more than seven years I have worked with authors and publishers on one problem: helping good books find their readers. Over that time I have marketed more than 380 books for over 280 authors.

My mission

To give every author the clarity, strategy and support to be seen, whether the book is brand new or has been waiting for attention for years.

My approach

I start by understanding your book, audience and goals, then build a plan around them. I keep you informed with plain-language updates and reports, and I adjust as we learn what works.

My values

Honesty about what marketing can and cannot do. Ethical practices that respect readers and platform policies. Clear communication. Care for your book.

Why authors choose me

Personalized strategy, a wide range of services under one roof, transparent communication, performance reporting and a free first consultation.
