My marketing process

A clear, eight-step path from first conversation to measured results.

  1. Initial Consultation

    We talk about your book, goals and any past marketing.

  2. Book and Author Assessment

    I review your book page, positioning, audience and current visibility.

  3. Marketing Strategy Development

    You receive a plan tied to your genre, stage and goals.

  4. Package Selection and Agreement

    We agree on scope, deliverables and a custom quote.

  5. Campaign Preparation

    Assets, copy, targeting and schedules are prepared for your approval.

  6. Marketing Execution

    The campaign goes live across the agreed channels.

  7. Performance Monitoring

    I track what is working and what is not.

  8. Reporting and Optimization

    You get clear progress updates and reports by email, and we adjust the plan.
